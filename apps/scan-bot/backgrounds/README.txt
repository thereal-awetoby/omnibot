Drop your own meme / art images here (.jpg .png .webp).
Every picture is used ONCE: after it appears on a PnL card (or /meme) it is moved to the "used" folder and never shown again.
The bot also downloads fresh crypto memes by itself (into the "cache" folder) and never downloads the same one twice.
When no fresh picture is available the card is plain, a picture is never reused.
Wide images work best.

YOUR OWN PICTURES (best): put the pictures you like in the folder  backgrounds\mine
The bot then uses ONLY those for PnL cards, one after another in random order (every picture is shown once before any repeats). Nothing is deleted.
You can also add pictures from Telegram: set ADMIN_IDS=<your telegram id> in .env, send the picture to any chat with the bot and reply to it with /pnlbg.
Wide pictures (16:9) look best. If the folder is empty, the bot falls back to downloaded memes.
